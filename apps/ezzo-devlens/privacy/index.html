Privacy Policy for Ezzo DevLens

Target App: Ezzo DevLens (com.ezzodevlens) | Last updated: October 2026

EzzoTechnology ("we", "our", or "the developer") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection and handling practices for the Android application "Ezzo DevLens" (the "Service").

1. Information Handling & Local Processing

Ezzo DevLens is designed as a standalone diagnostic utility. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on external servers.

  • Hardware & OS Data: Display metrics (DP/DPI/resolution), chipset specifications, OS build info, and battery status are retrieved locally using standard Android APIs and displayed strictly on your screen. This information is never sent to any external server.
  • Network Diagnostic Data: When you initiate diagnostic checks (Ping, DNS lookup, TLS handshake, HTTP status), requests are sent directly from your device to the target domain or IP you specify. Diagnostic outputs are stored only in memory for display or temporary clipboard copying.
  • Installed Package Query: Queried app package information (such as package names and version codes) is inspected locally on the device to assist with debugging. This list is never transmitted or uploaded.

2. Third-Party Services & In-App Purchases

Ezzo DevLens does not embed third-party tracking SDKs or ad networks in its core functionality.

If you choose to purchase the Pro version, financial transactions are handled securely and directly by the Google Play Store via Google Play Billing. EzzoTechnology does not have access to or store your payment details.

3. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Used exclusively to execute network tests (Ping/DNS/TLS/HTTP diagnostic checks) that you explicitly trigger, and to verify Google Play license status.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to determine whether the device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data to provide contextual network diagnostics.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
